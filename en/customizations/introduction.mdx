## 💬 Advanced Integrations

### WhatsApp Multi-Provider

Support for **3 different providers** for WhatsApp:

- **Evolution API** - Most stable and reliable
- **WAHA** - Open-source and self-hosted  
- **UAZ API** - Brazilian provider

Choose the provider that best fits your needs!

### Other Channels

- 📷 **Instagram Business** - Integrated direct messages
- 📘 **Facebook Messenger** - Complete support
- 📧 **Email** - Configurable IMAP/SMTP
- 💬 **Telegram** - Integrated bot
- 🌐 **Website Widget** - Chat on your site
